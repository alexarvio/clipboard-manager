Before you download

Will it slow my PC down?

No. It idles in the background listening for clipboard changes and does nothing until you copy something or press the hotkey.

Where is my clipboard data stored?

In a local database on your own machine. The only thing that ever leaves is a snippet you deliberately run through an AI transform or smart search — and it isn't logged at either end.

What happens when the trial ends?

If you don't cancel, your plan starts. Cancel during the trial and you're charged nothing and drop back to free — nothing in your history is deleted.

Do I need an account for the free version?

Yes — a free account with an email and password. It carries your plan across devices. Your history stays local either way.
