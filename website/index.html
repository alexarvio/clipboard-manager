How it works

Three steps to a clipboard that works for you

1

Copy like normal

FatClipboard runs quietly in the background and captures everything you copy — no setup, no extra clicks.

2

Find it instantly

Hit Ctrl+Shift+V, search or filter by category and date, and your snippet is right there.

3

Paste, organize, or transform

Drop it straight into your work, save it to a folder, or run it through AI first — then keep going.

04

Set it up the way you actually work

Pick your global hotkey, choose which AI presets and category filters show up, switch between light and dark, and keep an eye on your plan — all in one settings screen, organized into clear sections instead of one long list.
