BUT WINDOWS HAS ONE

"Can't I just use Win+V?"

You can, and for occasional use it's fine. Here's exactly where it stops.

Windows Win+V

Built into Windows, free

  • Already there, nothing to install
  • Optional sync across your devices with a Microsoft account
  • Stops at 25 items
  • No search at all — you scroll a small popup looking for it
  • Cleared every time you restart, unless you pinned it first
  • No folders, categories or tags
  • Stores screenshots, but can't search the text inside them
  • Paste it exactly as you copied it, or not at all

FatClipboard

Free tier, Pro from $3.99/mo

  • 100 items and 7 days free — unlimited history on Pro
  • Instant search as you type, plus AI search by meaning
  • Everything survives restarts, pinned or not
  • Folders, pins, and automatic categories for links, addresses and more
  • Reads the text inside screenshots so you can search it
  • Fix grammar, change tone, summarize or translate before pasting

Still not sure? The free tier covers all of the above except the AI features, forever. See a full breakdown of Windows clipboard manager options.

YOUR STUFF STAYS YOURS

Your clipboard never leaves your computer.

A clipboard manager sees everything — passwords, card numbers, private messages. So FatClipboard keeps your history in a database on your own machine, and there is no feature that uploads it.

Stored locally, always

Your history lives in a local database on your PC. There's no cloud copy, no backup on our side, and no sync.

We can't read it

Your account holds an email address and which plan you're on. It has never held clipboard content, and there's nowhere for it to go.

Screenshot text stays offline

Reading text out of your screenshots runs on your own machine using Windows' built-in OCR. No image is ever uploaded.

The one exception, stated plainly: AI features (transform and smart search) can't run offline. When you use one, that single snippet is sent to our server, passed to the AI provider, and the result comes back. It isn't logged or saved at either end — and if you never touch the AI features, nothing you copy ever leaves your machine.

QUESTIONS

Before you download

Will it slow my PC down?

No. It sits idle in the background listening for clipboard changes and does nothing until you copy something or press the hotkey. There's no indexing job, no background upload, and no scanning of your files.

Is my clipboard data sent anywhere?

Your history is stored only on your own machine and is never uploaded. The single exception is the AI features: when you run a transform or a smart search, that one snippet is sent to be processed and the result returned. It isn't stored. If you never use the AI features, nothing you copy leaves your PC. See the privacy section for the full detail.

Windows says "unknown publisher" when I install it. Is that normal?

Yes, and it's expected right now. Windows shows that warning for any app that hasn't been through paid code signing yet, regardless of what the app does. Code signing is on the way, which will remove the warning. Until then you can click "More info" then "Run anyway".

What happens when the 7-day trial ends?

If you don't cancel, the subscription starts and you're billed the plan you picked. If you do cancel during the trial, you're charged nothing and simply drop back to the free tier when it ends. Nothing in your history is deleted when you move back to free, though the free item limits start applying again.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes, from a self-serve billing page inside your account, in a couple of clicks. There's no phone call, no email to send, and no cancellation fee.

Do I need an account to use the free version?

Yes, a free account with an email and password. It's what carries your plan across devices later, and what lets you upgrade without re-entering anything. Your clipboard history itself stays local either way.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet, it's Windows only for now. You can leave your email below and you'll hear from us if and when a Mac build happens.
