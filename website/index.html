Before you download

Will it slow my PC down?

No. It idles in the background listening for clipboard changes and does nothing until you copy something or press the hotkey.

Where is my clipboard data stored?

In a local database on your own machine, and on the free plan nothing ever leaves it. Pro's AI features are the exception: an AI transform sends the item you run it on, a custom AI filter sends your 500 most recent items so it can pick the matches, and switching on Smart search sends your history to be indexed. All of it goes to our AI providers, Anthropic and Voyage AI. We record how many tokens each request used, for cost tracking; the text itself is never stored on our side.

What happens when the trial ends?

If you don't cancel, your plan starts. Cancel during the trial and you're charged nothing and drop back to free. Your history then follows the free cap again, so unpinned clips past the newest 50 or older than 7 days are trimmed — anything you pinned or filed into a folder is kept.

Do I need an account for the free version?

Yes — a free account with an email and password. It carries your plan across devices. Your history stays local either way.
